# Code of Conduct

Kin should be a welcoming place to build tools that connect people. Treat contributors and potential users with respect, including people of different ages, abilities, gender identities, sexual orientations, races, religions, cultures, and experience levels.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized comments directed at contributors, stalking, impersonation, and publication of another person's private information are not.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and project events. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or ban participants to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate and communicated with enough detail to explain the action while protecting people who report concerns.

Report ordinary moderation concerns to repository maintainers through GitHub. If a report contains private or sensitive information, use a private channel offered on a maintainer's profile or GitHub's reporting tools; do not put personal information in a public issue. A dedicated confidential project contact will be added before an organized community pilot.
