# Career connections with Kin

Career networking uses **Collaboration**. Two local policy agents compare deliberately selected goals and propose a small conversation. Both people independently decide whether to open human chat. An introduction does not verify qualifications, create a professional obligation or supply a job/funding offer.

## Try it with a willing adult tester

Use the updated browser app at [www.kinconnections.com](https://www.kinconnections.com), or current source installed with `npx --yes github:rudycelekli/kin-connect` (Node.js 22.19+, npm and Git). The pinned v0.2.1 release archive predates this feature. Keep both browsers open on the same relay; use separate browser profiles or devices so their identities stay independent.

In **My agent → Edit my agent**, continue to interests and use **Add your own interest** for a goal below. Review your values, availability and hard requirements, then save. Goals occupy the existing limit of 12 selected interests. You can add ordinary topics such as a field of work; Kin does not infer your occupation or credentials from them.

| One person selects         | The other person selects   | Suggested beginning                                        |
| -------------------------- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| `Career: peer learning`    | `Career: peer learning`    | Explore one question or practice exercise                  |
| `Career: find a mentor`    | `Career: offer mentorship` | Clarify a learning question, scope and boundaries          |
| `Career: explore jobs`     | `Career: hiring`           | Clarify the area of work and whether an actual role exists |
| `Career: find a cofounder` | `Career: find a cofounder` | Sketch a problem and small working experiment              |
| `Career: raise funding`    | `Career: investing`        | Explore a project question and information needs           |

These labels accept existing case, whitespace and Unicode normalization, but other wording does not activate a recognized goal. Select only the goals you actually want to disclose to a prospective peer. Two people both seeking a mentor, job or funding do not become complementary simply because they selected the same need. They remain eligible for an ordinary conversation if both policies allow it.

Open **Live network**, select **Collaboration**, and review the capsule. You can leave the goal unselected under public interests: it still reaches your chosen peer inside the encrypted policy card, but does not appear in the public directory capsule. Joining grants permission for that limited exchange. It does not share private requirements, notes or contact information. Relay metadata and capsule contents remain visible; see [privacy and consent](privacy-and-consent.md).

Refresh, select the other person's capsule, and choose **Let our agents talk**. Both agents check their own requirements, agreed availability, the complete genuine intersection, and the independently derived plan. Review the resulting career beginning. One approval keeps chat locked; both fresh signed approvals are required to unlock human chat. No agent can approve for you. Decline or block if you do not want the introduction.

## Current limits

Local/fictional discovery ranks eligible candidates using the explained career signal. Live directory selection stays owner-driven; private peer profiles are not used for automatic directory ranking. The brief beside the negotiated plan still uses shared common-ground prompts; the career-specific beginning appears in the actual meeting plan.

Both peers need updated agents for career-specific plan validation. Wire fields remain `kin/0.1`, but older clients can reject new career text rather than accept a plan they cannot derive. For multiple complementary goals, both agents use the same fixed priority: peer learning, mentoring, work exploration, cofounder exploration, funding. List order does not imply priority.

This feature adds no LinkedIn connection, background history collection, job application, automatic scheduling, verified club admission or payment flow. Pilot outcomes and professional usefulness remain unmeasured. The hosted service still requires monitored private support/abuse contacts and a supervised voluntary pilot before broad promotion; official ChatGPT installation and review are separate pending gates.
