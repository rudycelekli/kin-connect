Prototype policy · October 7, 2026

This draft describes intended use of the early reference implementation. It is not a finalized hosted-service agreement. An independent deployment needs its own reviewed operator terms and policies.

Use Kin with care

Kin is for adults aged 18 and over. Its input rule does not verify age or identity. Provide information you are entitled to share, use a public alias carefully, and respect other people's choices.

Do not impersonate others, create profiles for people without permission, harass, evade blocks, scrape capsules, or attempt unauthorized access. Do not use Kin to contact minors or to pressure anyone into approving an introduction.

Every hello needs two yeses

Circles use fictional organizers and local demo memberships. No live group membership, credential verification, paid admission, or group chat is provided. Saving a connection creates no invitation or new permission.

Agents recommend; each owner decides. An overlap score is not permission or a prediction of chemistry. No paid offering may override a hard requirement, decline, block, or the other owner's consent. Sharing another person's story requires their separate approval.

Kin does not automatically email people, import contacts, book appointments, or send messages outside the app. A meeting suggestion is not a reservation or a guarantee about the person you meet.

Know the prototype limits

There is no independent security audit, real-world identity verification, staffed moderation service, or guarantee of an introduction or relationship. Device private keys are plaintext local storage; metadata remains visible to the relay and there is no forward secrecy. Protect your device and review the privacy boundaries.

No billing or payment processing is implemented. The proposed business model charges for future managed hosting and community tools, not private data or another person's approval.

Open source and independent hosting

The code, client, and protocol reference are distributed under the repository's MIT license. Its license terms apply to the software. Independently hosted relays may have their own policies; choose a relay you trust.

For technical help or a security concern, use support. These draft guidelines may change as the prototype develops; check the release documentation.
